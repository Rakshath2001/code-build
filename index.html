venugopala-swamy-temple

---5.0 10 reviews

History : The Venugopala Swamy Temple located at Hosa Kannambadi, near Krishna Raja Sagara, is an example of Hoysala architecture in Karnataka, India. This temple was built in the 12th century AD around the same time as the Chennakesava Temple at Somanathapura, Mysore district. The Venugopala Swamy Temple, dedicated to a form of the Hindu god Vishnu, is located in Devanahalli Fort, a town in the Bangalore Rural district of Karnataka, India. It is a protected monument and showcases Dravidian-style architecture, with the temple being a part of the post-Vijayanagara empire
